### **Name: VachaVox**

**VachaVox** is a privacy-first, high-performance transcription engine designed to run locally. It is built for those who value **data sovereignty**—the belief that your words should never leave your machine.

## **Linguistic DNA**


| Component | Language            | Meaning                  | Concept                         |
| --------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| **Vacha** | Sanskrit / Gujarati | Speech, Voice, Utterance | The human, organic input.       |
| **Vox**   | Latin               | Voice, Sound, Tone       | The structured, digital output. |


**The Synthesis:** The name represents the transformation of fleeting human breath (**Vacha**) into permanent digital record (**Vox**).

---

## **The Mission Statement**

> *"Transforming sound into script, locally."*
> VachaVox provides a secure, "Zero-Cloud" environment where users can transcribe sensitive meetings, creative brainstorms, or technical notes without a subscription, an internet connection, or the risk of data harvesting.

- **Alias:** `vvox`
